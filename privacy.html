Privacy Policy — kongi-uploader

Last updated: 2026-10-12

kongi-uploader is a private tool operated by the creator of the "Injeolmi Kongi" character. It is used only to publish the creator's own original videos and images to the creator's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts on a schedule. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • Access tokens for the creator's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts, granted by the creator through each platform's official sign-in.
  • Basic account information of those same accounts (account name, channel name) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Upload status and links of the posts the tool publishes.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the creator's own content and to check that uploads succeeded. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer and in encrypted secrets of the creator's private automation. The creator can revoke access at any time from each platform's account settings (Google Account → Security → Third-party access; Instagram and TikTok app settings), after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Google API Services

kongi-uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
